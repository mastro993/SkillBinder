Page derivation tests (`snapshot_query`, on a test snapshot builder) cover a skill being Active when the active preset holds it for a detected agent or a workspace holds it, also one whose folder is missing, and Inactive when only another preset holds it, when the active preset holds it only for an agent that is not detected, or for All agents while none is; the preset passed in deciding rather than the snapshot's; All, Active, and Inactive partitioning the library in name order; Updates keeping only a newer version at the source, not a failed check or drift; each filter combining with the folder tab and search, each count applying the others, and the open popover's skill staying listed; a global skill directory linked to the universal one being one Universal entry naming every reader; All agents holding every entry; workspaces in sidebar order with the directories they hold, each directory a workspace held for all its agents uses, directories resolving to one folder sharing a pill, and a workspace without agents offering the detected agents' directories; and the switch giving All agents or the fixed list of the agents listed now, a pill off from All agents keeping the others, the last pill off emptying the preset's list or deactivating the workspace, and a pill of a workspace without agents choosing that directory. Control tests cover the popover opening below its trigger at its trailing edge, above it near the window's bottom, at a grid trigger's leading edge kept 8 points inside the window, and beside it when neither fits; agents taking their color logo, then their monochrome one, then the terminal glyph; grids fitting whole 180-point columns; and rows and tiles padding by half of what device-pixel snapping takes from the prototypes' 19.6-point name line (0.094 points at 2x, nothing at 1x); and weight 500 text laid out 100/110 of the way from its Regular width to its Medium one, as Chrome draws it from the system font's wght axis ("Inactive" at 12.5 points: 46.97), while Regular and Semibold keep their shaped widths. The exact line's tests (`controls/exact_text/tests.rs`) key that correction on the face GPUI resolves a weight to, with a stand-in text system that resolves the system font's faces as font-kit does: 501, 510, 520 and 530 draw Medium and are corrected, while 500, 530.5, 600 and another family's Medium are not. In a GPUI test window with those faces, weight 500 lines take the corrected width, a box sized ahead of layout as the Library filters' are takes the same, 500 keeps Regular's and Semibold its own; every line reaches the native-test bridge with its box and text, a labelled line is one accessibility label holding its text, and a Kit text button's text adds none beside the button's name; and a run whose glyphs are wider than its laid-out box stays whole, alone and under an ancestor that truncates, while a truncating name is cut to an ellipsis once its line is half its width. Render tests lay the controls out in a GPUI test window: the 26 by 24 pill, the 30 by 18 switch, the 28-point segment track with its 24-point segment, and four avatars 57 points wide also inside a plain wrapper; the popover's four agent pills ending at the trailing edge, eight wrapping onto two lines beside All agents at its full width, and twelve directory pills wrapping onto three lines beside a workspace's name; and the popover placed 4 points below a trigger painted in the same frame. Popover keyboard tests draw the popover's own content, box, and focus from a stand-in view in a GPUI test window: Tab and Shift-Tab cycling the All agents switch and every pill but a missing workspace's in drawing order, wrapping at either end, with only the focused control outlined; Enter and Space saving what a click on the same control saves; the controls keeping focus but saving nothing while the view is blocked, as it is while another operation runs or the progress strip is up; Escape handing focus back to the trigger whether the popover opened from the keyboard or a click; a click opening it with focus on the popover itself and no control outlined, a click on a pill focusing it without an outline, and the next Tab outlining the next pill; a press on the trigger, or a scroll after a click on a pill, closing it with focus on the view rather than the trigger; and the outline clearing each control, and an on pill's border, by 1 pixel. They register the app's key bindings through `skillbinder_ui::init`.
