# Library rows, filters, and where skills are on

Library lists every library skill unless the folder tab, the search, or a filter narrows it, and each row and tile shows where the skill is on: for which of your agents through the active preset, and in which workspaces ([ADR 0007](../../adr/0007-pages-list-active-skills.md)). A skill is Active when it is on right now: the active preset holds it for at least one of your agents, the detected agents, or at least one workspace holds it, even one whose folder is missing. A skill only presets that are off hold, or that the active preset holds only for agents that are not detected, is Inactive. Changing where a skill is on edits the active preset for your agents and the workspace itself for a workspace; copies follow as [Activation](activation.md) and [Workspace activation](workspace-activation.md) describe.

The design follows variant A, "Popover", with the Segmented filters, of the approved prototype, [`docs/design/scoped-pages/prototypes/library.html`](../../design/scoped-pages/prototypes/library.html); values below are logical points.

## Page frame and toolbar

Library frames itself: a 16-point gutter, 14 points above the toolbar, 12 between the toolbar and the list, and a list that scrolls under the fixed toolbar with 16 points below its end. The toolbar is one 32-point row, GPUI Kit's standard control height: the search field, the filters, a flexible gap, then the List and Grid segments.

- The search field grows from 120 points, never narrower than 100, sharing the free width with the gap. It holds a 15-point search glyph and the placeholder "Search skills", and has no clear button. Search matches the slug or description, ignoring case.
- The filters show on Library only; a workspace's page has none. All, Active, and Inactive are the segments of Kit's segmented tab bar, whose raised fill slides to the chosen segment; each label is followed by its count in 11-point medium tabular figures. The Updates chip is a round outlined toggle button with the upload-circle glyph and its count, with the tooltip and accessible name "Updates"; while on, it fills with the text color and its glyph and count take the page color. Tab reaches it and Enter or Space toggles it; only focus that a key gave it shows Kit's focus ring. Updates keeps the skills whose source has a newer version, the "Update available" state; drift and copies that need review are not updates.
- Each count applies the folder tab and the search: a segment's count also applies Updates, and the Updates count applies the chosen segment. It always shows, also at 0.
- Like the folder tab, the filters last the session: they stay while other pages are open, are not part of Back and Forward, and return to All with Updates off at launch. Changing one closes an open popover.
- List and Grid are the icon segments of a second segmented tab bar, with the tooltips "List" and "Grid". The choice is a machine-local preference, List by default, and survives restart.

When nothing is listed and a segment, Updates, or the search narrows the list, the page shows a search glyph in a 40-point rounded square, "No skills match", and a small outline "Clear filters" button, which sets All, turns Updates off, clears the search, and keeps the folder tab. A chosen folder with no skills shows the folder glyph and "No skills in <folder>".

## Rows and tiles

Skills are listed alphabetically by slug, ignoring case. List wraps every row in one card with a hairline border and 10-point corners on the list surface; rows are at least 56 points high, padded 10 by 14 points, and divided by hairlines inset 14 points at the top of every row but the first. A row shows the slug in 14-point semibold, ending in an ellipsis when the line has no room for it, its glyphs, and its folder as an 18-point tag while All skills is chosen and the list is at least 440 points wide, then one description line in 12.5 points; its placement area sits at the trailing edge, centred on the row. Grid shows tiles on the same surface with 10-point corners and 12 points of padding, in as many columns at least 180 points wide as fit, 10 points apart: the title line, two description lines, and a footer holding the placement area at its leading edge. Every line box is 1.4 times its font size, and weight 500 text takes the width Chrome gives it at that weight, between the system font's Regular and Medium faces.

An inactive skill's slug, tag, and description draw at half opacity; its glyphs keep theirs. The update glyph is the upload-circle in the info color, cyan, with the tooltip "Update available"; drift and validation glyphs follow it as [Library and organization](library-and-organization.md) describes. Clicking a row or tile outside its placement area and warning glyph, or Enter or Space while it has keyboard focus, opens the skill's page; right-click opens its menu, which keeps "Active in <preset>". Rows and tiles show the muted fill while hovered.

## Placement area

- An active skill's area is one button, transparent with 6-point corners, holding: the logo stack of your agents' skill directories that the active preset holds it for, Universal first with the layers glyph; a 1 by 14 point hairline when both your agents and a workspace show; one chip naming the only workspace that holds it, with the workspace's glyph or image, or, with two or more workspaces or a list narrower than 440 points (a tile narrower than 200), one chip with the code-folder glyph and their number; then an arrow-down chevron at 70% opacity.
  - Avatars are Kit's avatars as on a workspace's page: 18-point circles ringed 2 points in the fill behind them, the row's hover fill while it is hovered, and overlapping by 5; a color logo sits on white, clipped to the circle, a monochrome logo or glyph on the muted fill. Four show, then a "+N" circle.
  - The stack's tooltip is "Your agents", then "All agents" when the preset holds the skill for all of them, else their names. A workspace chip's tooltip is the workspace's name, then the directories it holds the skill in; the count chip's has one line per workspace, "<workspace> · <directories>".
  - The button has no tooltip; its accessible name is "Change where <slug> is on". Hovered or open, it shows the menu highlight, the text color at 8%, over the row's fill. While the popover is open the avatar rings take that fill too; while the pointer is on the button they keep the row's.
- An inactive skill's area is a 26-point circle with a dashed outline at 24% of the text color and a plus glyph, shown while its row or tile is hovered, it has keyboard focus, or its popover is open. Its tooltip is "Turn on", and its accessible name "Turn on <slug>".
- While any copy of the skill needs review, the warning glyph sits just before the area; its tooltip counts the copies and its click lists them in Skill copies.
- Clicking the area, or Enter or Space while it has focus, opens or closes the popover and never opens the skill's page. While another operation runs or the progress strip is up, the area does nothing and shows no hover highlight or hand cursor.

## Popover

The popover is 300 points wide with 12 points of padding and 12-point corners on the menu surface, with the menu's hairline ring and drop shadow. It opens 4 points below the area, lined up with its trailing edge in a list and its leading edge in a grid; when the window's bottom is too close it opens above the area, or above the whole tile in a grid, and it stays 8 points inside the window. Its accessible role is a dialog named "Where <slug> is on".

- **Your agents.** The label "Your agents" and, at its trailing end, the preset chip: a dot in the active preset's color, muted without one, and its name, with the tooltip "Saved to <preset>". Then "All agents" with a 30 by 18 switch, Kit's base switch whose shadowed thumb slides on Kit's spring, tooltip "Includes agents added later", and an icon-only pill per skill directory of your agents in Agents section order. Agents that read one directory, such as Universal or an agent whose global directory links to another's, are one pill; its tooltip is its name, then the agents that read it. Pills that do not fit wrap onto further lines at the trailing edge, and All agents keeps its width. With no agent detected, the pills give way to "No agents detected".
- **Workspaces**, under a hairline while any workspace exists: one line per workspace in sidebar order with its glyph or image and name, and at the trailing end its pills, one per skill directory it uses, directories resolving to one folder sharing a pill. A workspace that uses no agent yet reads "No agents yet" under its name and offers a pill for each detected agent's workspace skill directory. Pills that do not fit wrap onto further lines aligned to the trailing edge. A workspace whose folder is missing draws its line at 40% opacity with the tooltip "The workspace folder is missing.", and its pills do nothing.
- Pills are 26 by 24 points with round ends. Off: a dashed outline at 24% of the text color and the logo in grayscale at half opacity. On: a solid outline in the text color on the page fill, the logo in its own colors. Hover adds the muted fill.
- The switch on holds the skill for All agents, which includes agents detected later; off keeps exactly the agents detected now as a fixed list. A pill toggles its directory: from All agents, the others stay as a fixed list, and the last pill off removes the skill from the active preset without a dialog. A workspace pill toggles that directory for the workspace; the last one off removes the skill from the workspace. Choosing a pill of a workspace that uses no agent makes that directory its agent and turns the skill on there.
- Each change saves at once and the popover stays open, showing it; while it is open its row stays listed even if it no longer matches the filters, which apply again once it closes. While another operation runs or the progress strip is up, the switch and the pills do nothing, and show no hover or hand cursor. An edited managed copy still asks through Skill copies once the operation ends, and the popover hides while that dialog is open.
- A press outside it, Escape, scrolling the list, or leaving the page closes it.
- The keyboard reaches every control. Opening the popover focuses the popover itself, so no control shows a focus outline until Tab or Shift-Tab moves to one. They cycle through the switch and the pills, except a missing workspace's, in the order they are drawn, wrapping at either end instead of leaving for the page. Enter or Space acts on the focused control as a click does, and does nothing while another operation runs or the progress strip is up. Only a control focused from the keyboard shows a focus ring, Kit's on the switch's track and on the pills the prototypes' focus outline, a 2-point ring in the text color at 40%, 1 point outside the control and its border; a click focuses a control without one. When the popover closes while it has focus and the last input was a key, as with Escape, focus goes back to the trigger, so the keyboard is on the trigger again. A close after pointer input, such as a press on the trigger or outside it, or a scroll, focuses the window as before; a press then focuses whatever it lands on, as any press does. Whenever the focused element leaves the window, as the popover does on leaving the page or when its skill leaves the library, the window takes focus, so its shortcuts keep working.

## Text runs

Names, the `+N` avatar, and the popover's section labels, All agents and workspace names are each the app's exact line (`controls/exact_text.rs`), as are the progress strip's, the toast's and the review list's lines. Its box is as wide as Chrome lays the text out, kept to the fraction rather than rounded to the device pixel, so whatever follows lands where the prototype puts it. GPUI draws weight 500 with SF Pro's named Medium instance (wght 510 in the `fvar` table of `SFNS.ttf`), whose advances are wider than Chrome's weight 500 on the font's wght axis, so a line GPUI draws with the Medium face is laid out at Regular + 100/110 × (Medium − Regular) and drawn with Medium, a hair wider than its box. Which lines get it follows the face GPUI resolves a weight to, not the number: font-kit reads Medium at 530 and gives it every request above 500 up to 530, the app asks for 520 (`skillbinder_theme::MEDIUM`), and a request for exactly 500 draws Regular. Any other face keeps GPUI's shaped width. A line that may not fit, such as a skill's or a workspace's name, shrinks like a CSS flex item and ends in `…`; any other keeps its width. The segment counts, the Updates count, folder tags, and the workspace and preset chips' names and count are GPUI text with an id inside Kit's tab bar, toggle and tags, a label holding its text too. The segment labels, Clear filters and the other text buttons are Kit controls whose label is their accessible name.

Every exact line and every GPUI text with an id has that id in every build, so the `native-test` bridge reports its layout box and its text (`get_ui_tree`, `get_element_bounds`). The bridge names a run by its own id where that is unique in the window, and otherwise by the trailing ids of its path, such as `skill-row-pdf-tools.slug`. An exact line is also a label in the accessibility tree holding its text, except where an enclosing element's accessible name already reads that text: a row's or tile's name, and All agents beside its switch. Assistive technology reads each text once.
