# Sync: History

Part of [Synchronization](sync-and-durability.md#synchronization).

History (design Y6, without Restore yet; ADR 0038) lists the library's changes under the Sync page's other sections, while a remote is set and the library has a commit. It reads the library's Git history: each commit along the first-parent chain of the library's current commit, newest first. A Sync's merge takes the remote's commit as its first parent, so this chain is the published history on every device, and a merge lists what it brings against that parent alone. A commit not pushed yet shows too; Not synced yet also lists its skills.

Changes are grouped by the day they were made, in the computer's time zone, under a muted heading, "Today", "Yesterday", or a date such as "Wed, Nov 12", with its year when it is not this year, each day over one card. A change shows the device that made it, from its `SkillBinder-Device` trailer, with the "This device" tag when it is this device's name, or a clock glyph and "Before device names" for a commit without one (A4); under it, up to four chips, each a kind mark and a slug, then "+*n* more"; and at its end, its time as `HH:MM` and a chevron. A click, or Enter or Space, opens it in place to list each skill as a Not synced yet row: its kind mark, its slug, and why it changed, from the commit's `SkillBinder-Skill` trailer, or nothing for a commit made before device names. A skill whose newest change among those shown is this one, unless this one removed it, reads "Current version". Which skills a commit changed, and how, comes from its tree diff against its first parent, never from its trailers: a skill is added when its directory is new, removed when it is gone, and changed otherwise, including when only its record changed.

The engine reads History 50 changes at a time, and Show more, under the last day, reads the 50 after the last one shown (A3). It is a request, not part of the snapshot: the Sync page reads the newest 50 again whenever the library's commit, which the sync snapshot carries as `head`, has moved since they were read, which drops what Show more added, and each change that read brings flashes once. Without Git, or before the library's first commit, History is empty and hidden.
