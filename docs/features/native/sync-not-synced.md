# Sync: Not synced yet

Part of [Synchronization](sync-and-durability.md#synchronization).

Not synced yet (design Y5; ADR 0040) lists, under the Sync page's status and the remote's facts, per skill, what the next Push sends and what the next Pull brings, and is hidden while nothing waits. Both sides are measured from one base: the commit this device and the remote's commit share, or nothing when either has no commit, so connecting a library to an empty remote lists the whole library. This device's group, headed by its name, a "This device" tag, and "Not pushed", lists each skill that differs between the base and the files on disk, whether uncommitted or committed and not pushed, such as after a Push whose commit was made and whose network push failed. The remote's group, headed by the device that pushed it, or "Before device names" when its commits name none, and "*time* · Not pulled", lists each skill that differs between the base and the remote's commit. Each row shows a mark for its kind, `+` added, `~` changed, or `−` removed, by whether its directory is in the base and after the change; its slug; and why it changed (Y8): on this device, from the reason the change recorded, else from the newest `SkillBinder-Skill` trailer of the commits not pushed yet, else "Changed outside SkillBinder" or, for a removed skill, "Removed outside SkillBinder"; from the remote, from the newest trailer of the commits not pulled yet, and nothing for a commit made before device names; a restored skill reads "Restored to the version of *day*". Each of this device's rows ends with Discard, shown while the row is hovered or holds focus and inactive while other work or a Git operation runs; its tooltip says what it does. Discard asks first, "Discard your change to *slug*?", then puts the skill back as it was at the base (ADR 0040): a changed skill gets its base content and record back, a removed one comes back, unfiled and in no preset, and one added since the base leaves the library with its preset memberships and folder, while its managed copies stay where they are, unmanaged, as a removed workspace's do. It ends with the toast "Discarded the change to *slug*", and the row leaves the list.
